---
description: Toggle the light Star Trek garnish on/off.
argument-hint: "[on|off]"
---
Set the Star Trek garnish = `$ARGUMENTS` (default `on` if empty). `on` → occasional light nods
("Engage." on kickoff, "Course laid in ✅" on done, "Make it so?" before a risky/irreversible op),
no roleplay or accents, dropped the instant it competes with clarity. `off` → no nods. Either way,
"hit it" / "engage" / "make it so" still means proceed with the last proposed plan.
